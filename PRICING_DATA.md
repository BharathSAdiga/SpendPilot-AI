## Proposed Pricing Tiers

### Free (Seed Tier)
- Up to 10 SaaS tools tracked
- 1 audit report/month
- CSV upload only
- Community support
- **Price: $0**

### Starter
- Up to 50 SaaS tools
- Unlimited audit reports
- SSO integration (1 provider)
- Email alerts & renewal reminders
- 90-day spend history
- **Price: $49/month** | $470/year _(save 20%)_

### Pro
- Unlimited SaaS tools
- AI-powered spend recommendations
- Multi-SSO provider support
- Custom report branding
- 1-year spend history + trends
- Priority support
- **Price: $149/month** | $1,430/year _(save 20%)_

### Enterprise
- All Pro features
- Custom integrations (Jira, Slack, etc.)
- Dedicated account manager
- SLA guarantees (99.9% uptime)
- On-prem deployment option
- Custom data retention
- **Price: Custom** _(starting ~$500/month)_

## Notes

- Freemium is critical for bottom-up adoption in dev/startup teams
- Annual billing discount (20%) targets reducing churn
- Enterprise pricing to be set after first 2–3 inbound enterprise inquiries
- Consider usage-based pricing (per tool tracked) as an alternative model for scaling
