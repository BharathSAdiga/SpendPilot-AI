# GTM Strategy — SpendPilot AI

> Go-to-market plan, target segments, acquisition channels, and launch milestones.

---

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

| Attribute        | Description                                         |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| **Company size** | 10–200 employees (Seed to Series B startups)        |
| **Role**         | CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Finance, FinOps Lead   |
| **Pain**         | No visibility into SaaS spend or unused licenses    |
| **Trigger**      | Audit time, fundraise prep, cost-cutting initiative |

---

## Positioning

**One-liner:**  
*SpendPilot AI is the fastest way for startup teams to discover, audit, and optimize their SaaS subscriptions.*

**Value pillars:**
1. **Speed** — Full spend audit in under 5 minutes via CSV or SSO
2. **Intelligence** — AI classifies vendors, detects waste, prioritizes savings
3. **Simplicity** — No complex onboarding; built for operators, not procurement teams
