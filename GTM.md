# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## Exact Target Users
Our ideal customer profile (ICP) is the **Head of Engineering** or **Fractional CTO** at Series A-C startups (50-250 employees). These are teams heavily adopting AI tools (Cursor, Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, Anthropic APIs) but lacking centralized procurement. The pain point is decentralised credit card billing leading to "ghost seats" and shadow IT spend.

## Acquisition Channels
1. **Engineering-focused Social Content:** Deep-dives on X and LinkedIn analyzing the actual cost of "AI Tool Sprawl" based on aggregated anonymized data from our early audits.
2. **"Side-Project" Marketing:** SpendPilot AI itself acts as a free lead-generation tool. The core product is a hyper-valuable, instant audit. To get the full PDF report, users must enter a valid work email.
3. **Cold Email (Highly Targeted):** Scraping Crunchbase for recently funded Series A startups and emailing the VP of Engineering with a subject line: *"Are you overpaying for your Cursor/Copilot seats?"*

## Product Hunt Strategy
- **Launch Day:** Tuesday, 8:00 AM PST.
- **Hook:** "Stop wasting money on AI seats your team isn't using."
- **Offering:** 100% free instant audit for PH users, no signup required for the interactive dashboard.
- **Preparation:** Build a network of 50+ founders to secure early momentum in the first hour to hit the top 5 spots.

## Reddit Strategy
Avoid direct promotion; Reddit hates ads.
- **Target Subreddits:** r/SaaS, r/startups, r/devops
- **Tactic:** Publish a transparent "post-mortem" style post titled: *"We audited $50k in startup SaaS spend. Here are the 3 tools everyone overpays for."* Include a soft link to SpendPilot AI at the very bottom as the source of the data.

## X/Twitter Strategy
- **The "Build in Public" Play:** Documenting the journey of building SpendPilot AI's deterministic engine.
- **The "Tear-down" Play:** Thread analyzing the complex pricing tiers of Vercel or OpenAI and showing how easy it is to accidentally burn $1k/mo.

## First 100 Users Plan
- **Weeks 1-2:** Leverage personal network and YC/startup Slack communities (e.g., Latent Space). Offer free, 1-on-1 concierge onboarding where I walk through their audit results over a 15-minute Zoom call.
- **Goal:** Convert 10 of these early users into written case studies ("How Startup X saved $1,200/mo").

## Unfair Advantage
Our deterministic rule engine. While competitors rely entirely on expensive and slow LLM prompts that hallucinate pricing, our engine uses hardcoded, verified pricing registries. We can offer the initial audit completely for free at zero marginal cost, acting as a massive top-of-funnel magnet.

## Realistic Week 1 Traction Goals
- **Traffic:** 2,000 unique visitors (driven mostly by Product Hunt and Reddit).
- **Audits Run:** 500 completions.
- **Leads Captured:** 100 qualified B2B emails (20% conversion from audit to email).
- **Consultation Calls Booked:** 5.
